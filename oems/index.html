What your customers can do with it.

One copilot, many jobs. Each one draws on documentation you already have.

  • Train new operators

    Guided walkthroughs of the tool, from first power up to routine maintenance, at the operator’s pace.

  • Answer the common questions

    The questions your support team hears every week, answered instantly and consistently, with sources.

  • Check and tune recipes

    Look up recommended recipes, compare settings with the reference, and flag values that look off.

  • Troubleshoot faults

    Fault codes explained, likely causes ranked, and a checklist for the technician.

  • Commissioning checklists

    Step by step install and acceptance checks, with sign off recorded as each one is done.

  • Many languages

    Answers in the operator’s language, from one set of source documents.

  • Prepare field service

    Before a visit, your engineer sees the tool’s recent behavior and arrives with the right parts.

  • Parts and consumables

    Usage and wear tracked against the schedule, so replacements are planned, not rushed.

  • Safety reminders

    Lockout and handling steps shown at the moment they matter.

  • Demos and evaluations

    A working copilot to show prospects what owning your tool is like.
