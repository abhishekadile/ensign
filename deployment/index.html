Connect to what you have. Run where you decide.

Ensign 14 sits on top of the data your fab already collects. It reaches your tools through the interface each one supports, and it runs on your own hardware or in your own cloud account.

Tested on real equipment data.

Four ways in.

Most fabs use a mix. We start with what is already there and add only what a tool is missing.

  • Existing FDC systems

    If you already collect fault detection data, we read it. No second set of connections to the same tool.

  • SECS/GEM and OPC UA

    The standard equipment interfaces. Most modern tools speak at least one, so this is often the fastest route.

  • Tool side datalogging

    For tools that keep their own logs, we collect them at the tool, on the schedule that suits you.

  • Custom IoT

    Where a tool exposes little or nothing, we add sensors for the signals that matter. Older equipment included.

One view across mixed sources.

A fab rarely has one data path. Ensign 14 brings the paths together so a tool is judged on its own history, whichever way its data arrives.

Two ways to run it. You choose.

Both give you the same copilot. The difference is where it lives, and who looks after the machine it runs on.

On premises

Runs on hardware in your facility, on your network. Telemetry, logs and documents never leave the building.

In your AWS account

Runs in your own cloud account, in the region you choose. Your data stays in your account, under your controls.
